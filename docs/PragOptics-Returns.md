# PragOptics™ Returns Policy

**Version:** 2026-09  
**Effective Date:** Upon publication

---

## 1. What this covers

This policy covers physical products bought from pragoptics.com, such as the OmniSource. It does not cover donations, platform subscriptions, or preorder deposits, each of which has its own terms in the Subscriber Agreement.

A defect is not a return. If a unit fails, the warranty covers it: register the card that came in the case and use the redeem flow. The printed case is covered for life; the unit is covered for one year against manufacturing defects.

## 2. The window

You may return a product within **30 days of delivery**, for any reason.

## 3. Condition

The product must come back complete: the unit, the case, the harness, the accessories, and the warranty card, in the condition you received them. A unit that has been modified, damaged by misuse, or stripped of parts is not returnable, but may still be covered by the warranty if the fault is ours.

## 4. How to start a return

Email **support@bridgesindust.com** with your order number and the email you used at checkout. We reply with the return address. Return shipping is your responsibility; use a tracked service, since a package we never receive cannot be refunded.

## 5. What is refunded

- The price of the product, in full, to the original payment method.
- The original shipping charge is not refunded.
- Return shipping is not refunded.

Refunds are issued within five business days of the product arriving back with us. Your card issuer may take several more days to show it.

## 6. Partial refunds

If a return arrives incomplete or damaged, we may refund part of the price rather than the whole. We tell you what we found and what we refunded before the money moves.

## 7. Preorder deposits

A deposit on a product that has not shipped may be refunded on request before the unit ships. Once a preorder unit ships, it follows the same 30-day return window as any other product.
