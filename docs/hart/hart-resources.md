# HART Communication Resources

HART is the digital communication standard that rides on the 4 to 20 mA loop and connects technicians to smart field instruments. These guides cover how HART works and how to put it to work, from the protocol basics to communicators, calibration, HART-IP, and wireless. They are maintained by PragOptics, the maker of OmniBus, a universal HART communicator and calibration recorder.

- [What is HART?](/hart/what-is-hart/): how HART communication works, the 4-20 mA loop, masters, device variables, commands, DD/DTM, HART-IP, and calibration.
- [HART communicator](/hart/hart-communicator/): what a HART communicator is, the three form factors, and how to choose one.
- [HART calibration](/hart/hart-calibration/): As-Found / As-Left, trim types, and a step-by-step procedure.
- [HART-IP](/hart/hart-ip/): HART over Ethernet, gateways, and wireless mesh hosts.
- [HART vs 4-20 mA](/hart/hart-vs-4-20ma/): why HART rides on top of the analog loop instead of replacing it.
- [WirelessHART](/hart/wirelesshart/): the IEC 62591 mesh, gateways, and adapters.
- [HART commands](/hart/hart-commands/): universal, common-practice, and device-specific.
- [HART glossary](/hart/glossary/): key HART terms defined.
- [HART FAQ](/hart/faq/): common HART questions answered.
