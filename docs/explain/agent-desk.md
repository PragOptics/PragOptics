# Being a live agent

This is your side of Live Agents. A customer reads about bringing a real person
into their environment. You read about being that person, and getting paid for
it.

## The badge is yours, not a team's

Being an agent is a flag on your account, and only the platform owner grants it.
It is part of who you are on the platform, not a role on any one team. It unlocks
this internal Agents page on every environment you are in.

It does not make you an agent on anyone's team, it never changes your role on a
team you belong to, and it gives you no power over the platform owner's own
account. If the owner ever removes the badge, this page goes with it.

## The door and the case

A customer opens a door when they ask for a live hand. A case is one ask through
that door. Your open cases show up here: take one to come into that environment
as an agent, then switch to it at the top of the panel and get to work.

First contact is free. The first time you help a new customer, it costs them
nothing, so saying hello is never a bill.

## How you get paid

Your rate goes up to $75 an hour, and you set your own price for each customer:
a discount of up to half, a block of free minutes, or a full waive to work for
free. The customer always sees your rate before any billable time.

The money runs through the platform, and the platform pays you. It keeps 20
percent of what you charge, so you net 80 percent. At the full $75 rate that is
$60 an hour to you.

A customer's balance is prepaid and can never go below zero, so no one runs up a
bill they did not load. Your earnings, and what you are owed, show on your work
card once you are inside a customer's environment. There is a fuller breakdown
under How you get paid there.
