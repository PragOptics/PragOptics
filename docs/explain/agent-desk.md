# Being a live agent

This is your side of Live Agents. It is different from what a customer sees. A
customer reads about bringing a live person into their environment; you read
about doing that work and getting paid for it.

## The flag, not a team role

Being an agent is a flag on your account, granted by the platform owner. It is
part of who you are on the platform, not a role on any one team. It unlocks this
internal Agents page on every environment you are in. It never gives you power
over the platform owner's own account, and it does not change your role on any
team you belong to.

## The door and the case

A customer opens a door when they ask for a live hand. A case is one ask through
that door. You see the open cases you can answer here. Take one to join that
environment as an agent, then switch to it at the top of the panel to work.

First contact is free. If a customer's case has not been answered yet, the first
contact with them costs them nothing.

## How you get paid

The agent rate is up to $75 an hour. You set your price for each customer: a
discount up to half, free minutes, or a full waive. The customer sees your rate
before any billable time.

Money runs through the platform, and the platform pays you. The platform keeps
20 percent of what you charge, so you net 80 percent. At your full rate of $75
an hour, that is $60 an hour to you.

A customer's balance is prepaid and can never go below zero, so no one runs up a
bill they did not load. Your earnings and what you are owed show on your work
card when you are on a customer's environment.
