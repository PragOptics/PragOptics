# Being a live agent

This is your side of Live Agents, and only your side. A customer reads about
bringing a real person in. You read about being that person.

## Your badge, not a team's

Being an agent is a flag on your account, and only the platform owner grants it.
It is who you are on the platform, not a role on any one team.

```ladder
It unlocks | This internal Agents page, on every environment you are in.
It does not | Make you an agent on anyone's team, change your team role, or touch the owner's account.
Removed | If the owner takes the badge back, this page goes with it.
```

## Working a case

A customer opens a door; a case is one ask through it. Your open cases show up
here.

```flow
See a case | On your desk here.
Come in | Take it, join that environment.
Do the work | Switch to it, build.
Log your time | It bills to their balance.
```

First contact is free. The first time you help someone new, it costs them
nothing, so saying hello is never a bill.

## Getting paid, in short

You set your price for each customer, up to $75 an hour, and you keep 80 percent
of what you charge. The full breakdown, the split and your running total, is
under How you get paid on your work card once you are inside a customer's
environment.
