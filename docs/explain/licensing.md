# Licenses and mailboxes

Every team seat can have a mailbox, included in your plan from the User plan up. Any other Microsoft 365 license is bought here at Microsoft's list price per license seat, plus sales tax, charged to the owner's card before it is ordered, each license on a bill of its own beside your plan. PragOptics is your Microsoft partner: it orders your licenses through Microsoft's distributor and runs the Microsoft side for you.

## Who does what

The owner decides everything on Licensing and approves every purchase. On the Team tab the owner can give a role more:

```flow
Ask for purchases | Asks the owner to buy a license, add or remove license seats, or end a license. Nothing is bought until the owner approves it.
Give out mailboxes and licenses | Gives and takes back team seats' mailboxes, gives bought licenses to people on the team, and brings every seat up to date in your Microsoft tenant. Buys nothing.
Connect your Microsoft tenant and set up its mail | Connects your Microsoft tenant, adds your verified domains to it and switches your mail to Microsoft.
Accept agreements | Accepts the Microsoft Customer Agreement for your business.
```

Everyone else in a team seat (an admin, a developer or a member) sees Licensing and reads it. Nobody has to ask for a mailbox: the owner, or someone whose role has Give out mailboxes and licenses, gives it. A team member who has none yet can still ask the owner for it here. Viewers do not see Licensing. A request waits under Requests for you on the owner's Licensing tab, the owner is emailed about it, and the person who asked is emailed the answer.

## The licensing account

Before the first license, your business needs a licensing account. The owner creates it once, under your business's legal name, not a person's name. When your billing details on Billing carry a business name, the name box starts with it; the owner checks it, changes it there if it is wrong, and confirms it before anything is created. It uses the billing address you gave on Billing, and nothing is charged. The business name can be changed later on the same card; that change does not touch your billing details.

## Your Microsoft tenant

Your licenses and mailboxes live in a Microsoft tenant: your business's own space at Microsoft. The owner names a new one, 3 to 27 letters and digits, which becomes that name followed by .onmicrosoft.com, or gives the ID of a tenant your business already has. A name Microsoft already has is refused, and the card says so under the box.

Once mail or a license is ordered, the tenant's name is fixed. It can change again only if that order is refused, or cancelled before Microsoft makes the tenant. A new tenant is set up by PragOptics with the first order, whether that order is mail or a license; the card says so until Microsoft has made it, then shows the tenant's name and ID. While it is being made, nothing else can be ordered for it; the card says when it is ready.

A tenant PragOptics made is yours. PragOptics keeps its own administrator account in it so your licenses and mail keep working, and you never need that sign-in. Once Microsoft has made the tenant, the owner can ask on the same card for an administrator account of their own, with full control of the tenant. PragOptics makes it, and you are emailed when it is ready. Its first password then waits on the card: press Show the first password, and it is shown to you once and deleted, so keep it somewhere safe until you sign in. It is never sent by email. Microsoft asks you to choose a new password the first time you sign in at admin.microsoft.com.

## Connecting your tenant

Before PragOptics can make mailboxes in your tenant, add your domains to it or switch your mail, the tenant is connected: its administrator approves PragOptics once, for the organization. A tenant PragOptics made is connected by PragOptics, and the card says so; nothing is needed from you.

For a tenant your business already had, the owner, or someone whose role has Connect your Microsoft tenant and set up its mail, presses Connect your tenant. You sign in at Microsoft as an administrator of that tenant, Microsoft shows its approval page for that same organization, you approve, and you come back to the card. The link works once and for ten minutes. A sign-in to a different organization than the one named on the card, or an approval for one, connects nothing, and the card says so. One Microsoft organization connects to one team.

Connected, the card shows the organization, the licenses it holds at Microsoft and how many are in use, and each of your verified domains. If your administrator ever removes PragOptics from the tenant, the card says so and offers Connect it again.

## Your domains and your mail

Each domain you verified on the Domains tab can join your tenant here. Add to tenant asks Microsoft for its proof record: where PragOptics manages the domain's DNS the record is written for you; otherwise the card shows it with Copy, you add it where the DNS is managed, and Check again asks Microsoft to look. Once the domain is in the tenant, new mailboxes can use it.

Switching a domain's mail to Microsoft is its own step. Switch mail to Microsoft first shows exactly what changes: the mail records written, an existing sender record merged rather than doubled, and where the domain's mail goes today. Then you confirm. Where the DNS is managed elsewhere, the records are shown for you to add, and I have added them records the switch once Microsoft's mail server answers for the domain. Mail already in the old mailboxes stays there until it is moved.

## Your own mailbox

Once your seat has a mailbox and the tenant is connected, a card of your own, My mailbox, shows your address and where it stands. If a new Microsoft account was made for you, your first password waits there for you alone: Show my first password shows it once and deletes it, so keep it somewhere safe. Microsoft asks you to choose your own password the first time you sign in at outlook.office.com. Nobody at PragOptics, and no administrator on your team, can see or set your password; if you lose it, Reset my password sets a new first password that waits on the same card, once.

## The agreement

Microsoft asks someone at your business to accept its Customer Agreement before anything is ordered. The owner, or someone whose role has Accept agreements, accepts it while signed in: their name and email are recorded from the account they are signed in with, never typed. A person whose account has no first and last name yet adds them on Profile first. It stands while that person is on the team and their role keeps the switch. If they leave, are suspended or lose it, someone who has it accepts again before the next order.

## A mailbox for every team seat

The owner turns on mail for the team, and nothing is charged; the owner's own mailbox comes first. Then each team seat can be given its mailbox. No domain is needed: each mailbox starts on your tenant's name, name.onmicrosoft.com, or on a domain of yours whose mail was switched to Microsoft. Every included mailbox is Exchange Online Kiosk, the same on every plan: 2 GB, for Outlook on the web and the Outlook phone apps, not the desktop Outlook app. A mailbox that has been given reads "Waiting for your Microsoft tenant" until the tenant is connected; then the person is made in the tenant and given the license, at once or on the hourly check, and the card shows their address. A person whose own address is already on a domain in your tenant keeps that account and signs in as always. A mailbox ends when it is taken back, when its seat ends, or when the person becomes a viewer. Once a mailbox ends, Microsoft keeps its mail for 30 days, then deletes it; the person can no longer sign in to an account PragOptics made.

The included mailboxes follow your plan's term. On a monthly plan they are month to month. On a yearly plan they are on Microsoft's 1-year term, counted from the day mail was turned on; if your plan ends or the account closes before that term does, the rest of it is charged to your card, because Microsoft bills it to the end.

## Which license does what

```flow
Exchange Online (Plan 1) | A larger mailbox with an archive, for a person who needs more than the included one.
Microsoft 365 Business Basic | A mailbox plus Teams and the web versions of Word, Excel and PowerPoint, with OneDrive storage.
Microsoft 365 Business Standard | Business Basic plus the Office apps installed on the desktop.
Microsoft 365 Business Premium | Business Standard plus device management and advanced security.
```

A license without a mailbox, such as Copilot or Visio, leaves the person's included mailbox as it is. A license that has its own mailbox, such as those above, takes the place of the included one for that person, and the included one is freed.

Some licenses need a base license: Microsoft sells them only to a business whose Microsoft tenant already has one, and the license says which. When none was bought through PragOptics, whoever adds it or asks for it ticks that the tenant already has one; nothing is charged until then. Each new license is checked with Microsoft's distributor before the card is charged, and one refused at that check charges nothing.

Microsoft sells education licenses only to schools and colleges it has approved, and nonprofit licenses only to organizations approved in its nonprofit program. On those licenses the owner asks for Microsoft's approval instead of adding them; approval usually takes up to three business days, and the owner is emailed the answer. Government licenses are not sold through PragOptics.

Prices on the Licenses card are Microsoft's list price per license seat. Sales tax at your billing address is added to every charge, and the exact amount, tax included, is on the button before you press it. Each license has its own plan, chosen when it is added from what Microsoft offers for it on your plan's term: on a yearly plan, licenses are bought on a 1-year term; on a monthly plan, month to month. A bought license is given to a person on the team on Your licenses; its license seats say how many people can hold it.

## How a license is billed

Adding a license charges the owner's card first: the license seats times Microsoft's list price for the plan, plus sales tax. The exact amount is on the button. Only when that charge has gone through is the license ordered, so nothing is ordered that has not been paid for. A declined card orders nothing.

Each license is billed on its own from the day it is bought, and renews on that day each period until it ends: the same day Microsoft renews it.

- More license seats are charged now, for the rest of the current period, and apply now. The exact charge is on the button.
- Fewer license seats take effect on Microsoft's next renewal date for that license. The date is shown on the license, and you keep paying for the dropped license seats until then, and for the day or so after it that Microsoft takes to apply the change, which is added to that license's next bill.
- Ending a license takes effect at the end of what was paid for. A license on a commitment runs, and is billed, to the end of its commitment, even if you end it sooner, because Microsoft bills it for the whole commitment. It stays usable until it ends.
- If a payment fails, it is tried again over the following days and you are told. If it never goes through, the license ends when Microsoft ends it, at the end of its current term, and the rest of that term is charged to your card, because Microsoft bills it to the end; a license on a yearly commitment is charged the rest of the year. If that charge fails too, it is owed: the Licensing tab shows it with Pay now, and new licenses wait until it is paid.
- If Microsoft changes its list price for a license you hold, the new price applies from that license's renewal after Microsoft's change. You are told by email at least 30 days before when we learn of it that early, and as soon as we learn of it when Microsoft's change reaches us later.
- If Microsoft does not complete an order or more license seats after your card was charged, our team looks into the charge and contacts you.

What you were charged for a license Microsoft delivered is not refunded and is not turned into a credit.

## While paused, or after the plan ends

While your environment is paused, or once your plan has ended, Licensing is read only: you can see every license, mailbox and request, and the date each license ends, and nothing is bought or changed. The owner can still decline a request that was waiting.

## What you are not asked to do

You do not buy from Microsoft yourself or manage licenses in a Microsoft portal. What you see on Licensing is the whole of it: the account, the tenant, the agreement, the licenses, the mailboxes and the requests.
