# Running live agents

Your view as the platform owner. This is the whole program, end to end: who your
agents are, every request across the platform, and the money that runs through
you.

## The three levels

```ladder
You, the owner | See everything: the roster and every case. Hand out the agent badge. Your own work is never split.
Your agents | Each holds the badge and works their own open cases. They see only their side.
The customer | Opens a door, requests a hand, controls the relationship. Sees only their side.
```

## The roster is yours to set

An agent is anyone you give the agent badge. It is a flag on their account, not
a seat and not a team role, and only you grant or remove it.

```flow
Grant the badge | From the Team tab or Users desk.
They appear here | On your roster, with their earnings.
Remove any time | The badge comes off, their account stays.
```

## A request, end to end

```flow
A door opens | A customer requests a hand.
You or an agent | Take it, come into their environment.
The work is logged | It draws from their prepaid balance.
The platform bills | The customer, and pays the agent.
```

## The money

Every charge runs through the platform. The platform keeps 20 percent of an
agent's charge and pays them the other 80 percent. Your own work as the owner is
not split, because you are the platform. A customer's balance is prepaid and
never goes below zero, so no one is billed past what they loaded.
