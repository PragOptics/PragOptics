# Orders and tracking

An order moves through a few states, and you hear from us at the ones that matter.

```flow
Paid | Your card settled. You get the confirmation with the order number.
Labeled | A shipping label exists. Nothing has moved yet, so nothing to tell you.
Shipped | The carrier scanned it. You get the email with the tracking number.
Delivered | The carrier says it arrived. You get the email.
```

The tracking number and the carrier's latest word show here under each order, and on the Track page if you ordered as a guest. The carrier's status updates automatically; you never have to refresh anything.

## When something goes wrong

If the carrier reports a problem, a failed delivery attempt or a package coming back to us, you get an email that quotes what the carrier said, and so does our team, at the same moment. A returning package is our problem to sort out with you; write support with the order number and we will send it again.

## Refunds

A refund shows on the order as soon as it is issued and goes back to the card you paid with. Partial refunds are noted as partial. The Returns policy at the bottom of the page is the rule.

## Guest orders

An order placed without an account stays on the email you used at checkout. You can always look it up on the Track page with the order number and that email. If you make an account later, link the order here with its order number; we never attach an order to an account silently by matching addresses.

## Order numbers

Every order number is a long random id from the confirmation email. It is the key to tracking, claiming and support, so keep the email.
