# The Needs attention desk

Everything the platform could not settle on its own, across every customer, lands here for an operator to decide. You get one email when an item is new or comes back, never one per repeat.

## What shows up here

- Orders, companies, tenants and subscriptions that stalled, were refused, or lost the line behind them.
- Endings and pauses that did not go through after the retries.
- A Microsoft 365 connection waiting on an administrator's approval.
- A site or an account reported against the community standards.
- An owner's request for an administrator account.
- An amount still owed after a closure for cause, and published files a closure could not delete.
- Owners an agreement notice could not reach.

## How an item clears

Each item offers what can be done about it.

```flow
Press an action | you choose what to do about the item
Platform finishes it | the item resolves and leaves the list, with its note
It cannot finish | the item stays here, showing the answer it came back with
```

**Approve at Microsoft** opens Microsoft's approval page in this tab. Sign in there with that tenant's administrator account, and Microsoft sends you back here, where the line above the list says what Microsoft answered.

Every press is recorded on the item under History, and the Resolved tab lists what was settled, by whom, and when.
