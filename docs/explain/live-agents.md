# Live Agents, not AI

A real person, not a bot. When you want a hand building or fixing your
environment, you bring in a live human from the PragOptics team to work on it
with you. The little alien is just a stand-in until that person adds a photo.

You hold the door. Nobody comes in unless you ask.

```flow
Open the door | Press Request agent support.
Someone comes in | You see who, and their rate.
They build with you | Inside your environment, with you.
You show them out | One press, any time.
```

## What it costs

Your first contact is free. After that you keep a small prepaid balance, and an
agent's time draws from it. You spend only what you have added, and it can never
drop below zero, so a bill never surprises you.

```ladder
Platform owner | $150 an hour.
An agent | $75 an hour, and they may discount it or work for free.
You decide | Set a spend cap, top up when you like. Low balance pauses paid work.
```

Whoever comes in shows you their rate first, and every minute they log lands in
your ledger with what it cost.

## What an agent can and cannot do

```ladder
They can | Connect accounts, set up your domain and mail, run licensing, do the developer work.
They cannot | Touch your billing, buy a domain, close your account, change your team, or read a password or secret.
```

Every action is recorded against their name and your environment, so you always
know what was done, and by whom.
