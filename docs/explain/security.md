# Your sign-in

Every sign-in takes two steps. A password alone never opens the account, not even ours.

```flow
Password | Proves you know it. Wrong five times and the account pauses for a while.
Second step | Proves you have something: a passkey on a device you own, or a code from your authenticator app.
Session | Lasts an hour of inactivity. While you are working it renews itself; walk away and it lapses.
```

## Passkey or authenticator

Either one completes the second step. You are never asked for both.

A **passkey** is your fingerprint, face, or device PIN. It lives on the device and answers only pragoptics.com, which is what makes it phishing-proof: a look-alike site cannot ask it anything. Add one from this card; you confirm with your password first.

An **authenticator app** shows a six-digit code that changes every thirty seconds. Setting one up shows a QR code once. If you change phones, use Reset authenticator here to set up the new one.

## Recovery codes

The first time you set up either kind of second step, you get recovery codes. They are shown once. Each works one time and gets you in when the passkey and the app are both gone. Keep them somewhere that is not the phone.

If you lose the second step and the recovery codes, there is one way back, through support, and it is deliberately slow and recorded.

## Signing out everywhere

Changing your password, resetting your second step, or closing the account signs out every device at once. So does an operator suspending the account. You will see a plain message that the session ended, and you sign in again.

## What we never do

We never ask for your password by email, text, or phone. We never send a sign-in link that bypasses the second step. Support cannot see your password or your codes; nobody can.
