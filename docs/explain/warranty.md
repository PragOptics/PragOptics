# Registration and transfers

Every unit ships with a card and a code. Registering ties that code to you. It is free, it needs no account, and it is what makes the warranty yours.

```flow
Register | The code and the email you want on record. That is the whole form.
Covered | The printed case is covered for life. The unit is covered one year against manufacturing defects.
Redeem | A covered case is replaced from this page. You pay shipping only.
Transfer | Passing the unit on? Ownership, warranty included, moves to the new email. Once.
```

## With an account, or without

Registration stands either way. An account keeps every unit you own in one place, under My Products, so you can redeem or transfer without digging out the card. Adding an account later links the units you registered with the same email.

## Transfers

A transfer sends a confirmation to the new owner's email. Nothing moves until they accept. When they do, the registration, the coverage and the code are theirs, and your record shows the unit as transferred. The email you registered with is the key to starting one.

## Replacement cases

A replacement ships to the address you give at that moment and is paid for by you at the shipping rate shown, nothing more. The new case comes with a new card code, already tied to you.

## What the code is not

The code is not a serial number and not a password. It identifies the unit for warranty only. If you lose the card, the registration email is enough for support to find it.
