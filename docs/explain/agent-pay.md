# How you get paid

The money detail, from your side. A customer pre-loads a balance; your logged
time draws from it at your rate; the platform carries the billing and pays you.

## The split

You keep 80 percent of what you charge. The platform's fee is 20 percent.

```flow
You charge | $75 an hour.
Platform fee | 20 percent, $15.
You net | $60 an hour.
```

## Your price is yours

No one else sets it. For each customer you choose:

```ladder
Discount | Up to 50 percent off your rate.
Free minutes | A block of time at no charge.
Waive | You work for free.
```

The customer sees your rate before any billable time. Waived time and free
minutes earn nothing, by your choice. Every customer's first contact is free, so
the first session you log for someone new is not charged and does not earn.

## What draws, and what does not

Only logged session time draws from the balance, and the balance can never go
below zero, so a customer is never billed past what they loaded. If it runs out
mid-session, the charge caps at what is left. Your running total, earned and
owed, shows on your Agents desk.
