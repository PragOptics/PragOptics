# How you get paid

You are on the PragOptics agent roster. When a customer asks for a live hand and you take it, you work in their environment and log your time. Here is how the money works, from your side.

## The split

A customer pre-loads a balance, and your logged time draws from it at your rate. The platform handles the billing, the payments and the customer relationship, and pays you for your hours **less a 20% service fee**.

So you keep **80%** of what you charge. An hour at the $75 agent rate is **$60 to you, $15 to the platform**. Your earnings add up as you work and are shown as owed to you.

The platform owner's own work is not split: the owner is the platform.

## Your price is yours to set

For each customer you choose your own price, and no one else sets it:

- A **discount** of up to 50% off your rate.
- **Free minutes**, a block of time you give that customer at no charge.
- A full **waive**, you work for free.

The customer sees your rate before any billable time. Time you waive or give as free minutes earns nothing, that is your call. And every customer's **first contact is free**, so the first session you log for a new customer is not charged and does not earn.

## What draws and what does not

Only time you log in a session draws from the balance, and the balance can never go below zero, so a customer is never billed more than they loaded. If their balance runs out mid-session, the charge caps at what is left; add-on top-ups are theirs to make.

Your running total, earned and owed, shows on your Agents desk.
