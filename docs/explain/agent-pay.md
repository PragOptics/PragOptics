# How you get paid

You are on the PragOptics agent roster. When a customer opens their door and you
come in, you work in their environment and log your time. Here is how the money
works, from your side.

## The split

A customer pre-loads a balance, and your logged time draws from it at your rate.
The platform carries the billing, the payments and the customer relationship, and
pays you for your hours less a 20 percent service fee.

So you keep 80 percent of what you charge.

```flow
You charge | $75 an hour, your full agent rate.
Platform fee | 20 percent, which is $15.
You net | $60 an hour.
```

The platform owner's own work is not split: the owner is the platform.

## Your price is yours to set

For each customer you choose your own price, and no one else sets it.

```ladder
Discount | Up to 50 percent off your rate.
Free minutes | A block of time you give that customer at no charge.
Waive | You work for free.
```

The customer sees your rate before any billable time. Time you waive or give as
free minutes earns nothing, and that is your call. Every customer's first contact
is free too, so the first session you log for someone new is not charged and does
not earn.

## What draws, and what does not

Only time you log in a session draws from the balance, and the balance can never
go below zero, so a customer is never billed past what they loaded. If it runs
out mid-session, the charge caps at what is left, and topping up is theirs to do.

Your running total, earned and owed, shows on your Agents desk.
