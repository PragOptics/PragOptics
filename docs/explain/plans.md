# Which plan fits

Start from what you are trying to do, not from the price.

```ladder
Super | You run the platform at scale, for many people, with forty-five seats and the highest limits.
Partner | You build on PragOptics for other people. Five seats for your team, publishing to the builds marketplace, and your commerce stays yours: your customers are yours, served by what you build.
User | Your own environment: storage, API access with your own keys, up to five connected domains, a mailbox, and add-ons when you need more.
Free | Every owner. Warranty registration, all the software, the docs and print profiles, the API console with limited allowances, verified builds from the board.
```

## The honest version of each

**Free** is a real plan, not a trial. If you own the hardware, everything about owning it is free forever: registering it, transferring it, running the software, downloading what other people have built. You get an environment the moment you sign in, on shared storage with the Free allowance, so the software has somewhere to keep your things from day one. You never enter a card.

**User** is where the platform becomes yours. You get an environment provisioned the moment the subscription settles, with storage behind it, keys for your own programs, and up to five domains you own connected to it. One seat. If you outgrow the storage or the API allowance, the two add-ons raise them.

**Partner** is for building for others. Five seats for your team, the ability to publish what you build, and the arrangement that matters: what you build serves your own customers, through your own connected accounts, and the money they pay you is yours. They are your customers, not accounts on PragOptics.

**Super** is Partner at scale: forty-five seats and the highest limits on the platform. If you are asking whether you need it, you probably do not yet.

## Things that are the same on every paid plan

Domains and hosted mail are User and up, not Partner and up. Connecting a domain you own is free: up to five on User, twenty on Partner, fifty on Super. Registration through us is passed through at the registrar's cost, with no markup. A mailbox comes with every seat. The owner, or someone the owner allows, gives it, so nobody has to ask, and it needs no domain: it starts on your Microsoft tenant's own name.

## Changing your mind

Upgrades take effect now and charge the difference. Downgrades wait for the end of the period. Nothing refunds. The full rule is on the Billing page under How it works.
