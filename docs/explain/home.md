# Your environment home

One page per environment, and the first thing you land on: everything this environment holds, each with the way in. Switch environments above and this page follows.

- **Projects** — your websites and the reusable parts (forms and apps) they are built from. A project has one home; open it in the Studio.
- **Published sites** — what this environment has published, at its own address. A site from one of your projects shows on that project; anything here was published without a project.
- **Data** — the tables of data this environment holds. The Studio, your site and your API keys all read and write them.

Every list here is a read the sections already make; this page just gathers them and sends you to the section that works on each.
