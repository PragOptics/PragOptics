# PragOptics™ Shipping Policy

**Version:** 2026-09  
**Applies to:** physical goods purchased from the PragOptics store, operated by **Bridges Industrial LLC** (“BI”).

---

## 1. Where We Ship

We currently ship to addresses in the **United States**.

International shipping is not yet available at checkout. If you are outside the US, email **support@bridgesindust.com** and we will quote your order by hand.

---

## 2. Rates and Carriers

Shipping is carried by commercial carriers (for example USPS and UPS). At checkout you are shown **live carrier rates** for your address and cart, and you choose the service you want.

The amount you are charged for shipping is exactly the rate you selected. Rates are verified server-side against your order's own address and contents at the time of purchase; a rate can never be substituted for a different one without your agreement.

---

## 3. Processing and Tracking

Orders ship after payment settles. When your shipping label is created you receive a tracking number, and signed-in customers can follow their order and its tracking from the **Orders** section of their account at any time.

Delivery timing is the carrier's estimate for the service you selected and is not a guarantee by BI.

---

## 4. Preorders

A preorder deposit reserves your unit. Nothing ships at deposit time, so no shipping is charged with the deposit. Shipping is quoted and charged with the balance when your unit is ready to ship. Deposits are fully refundable until the balance is charged.

---

## 5. Address Accuracy

Shipping labels are purchased for the address you provide at checkout. Please check it carefully. If a package is returned to us because of an address error, contact **support@bridgesindust.com** and we will work out reshipment with you; carrier fees for reshipment may apply.

---

## 6. Damaged, Lost, or Missing Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, or tracking shows it lost or stalled, contact **support@bridgesindust.com** with your order details. We will pursue the carrier claim and make it right with you.

Device defects and case coverage are handled under the product's warranty terms, separate from this shipping policy.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

BI may update this policy as carriers, coverage, and services change. The version published here is the one in effect.
