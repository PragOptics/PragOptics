# PragOptics Open Hardware, Warranty & Liability

*Applies to every PragOptics hardware product — the open designs we publish and the assembled units we sell. This is written to be read, not to scare you off. It lays out exactly what we stand behind and where responsibility sits, so you can buy or build with confidence.*

---

## The idea: buy it, or build it

Every PragOptics hardware product is published as an open, proven design **and** offered as a finished unit you can buy.

- **Build it.** We publish the full design — schematic, bill of materials, assembly notes, and enclosure files — free. Build your own from proven, known-good parts, as many as you like, for your own use. Providing the exact parts and settings is the point: it keeps the hardware accessible.
- **Buy it.** Don't want to source parts and solder? Buy one assembled, tested, and set by us. A unit we build is covered by the limited warranty below; a unit you build is not.

We would always rather you buy one — it supports the work and comes with our warranty — but the design is yours either way.

## Open-hardware license (the plain version)

The published designs are for **personal, non-commercial** use. You may build them for yourself, as many as you like, and share the link. You may **not** sell units or plans built from our designs, reproduce them commercially, or use the PragOptics name or product marks on a competing or derivative product. The full personal-build license ships with each download and governs in full.

## Limited warranty (units we manufacture)

**Hardware built and sold by PragOptics is warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for one (1) year from the date of delivery.** If a unit we built fails from a manufacturing defect within that period, we will, at our option, repair or replace it. That is the remedy. Separately, the **printed case is warranted for life** — if a case we supplied cracks or fails, we will replace or reprint it free, for as long as you own the unit. **Coverage requires that you register the unit within 30 days of purchase** — see *Activating your warranty* below.

This warranty covers **only** units built and sold by PragOptics. It does **not** cover:

- units you or anyone else built from the published designs;
- damage from misuse, accident, modification, unauthorized service, or use outside the product's documented intended use and safety envelope;
- normal wear or consumables (for example, a worn test lead or cable);
- any device connected to, integrated with, or modified by third-party parts, accessories, or software we did not supply.

To request warranty service, contact PragOptics with proof of purchase. **Shipping is not included** — you cover shipping to us for warranty service; we cover the repair or replacement itself.

## Activating your warranty

Every unit we build ships with a **warranty card** carrying a **unique registration code** — one code, tied to one device. To activate the warranty above (the one-year defect cover and the lifetime case cover), **register your unit within 30 days of purchase** using the code on the card.

- **One code, one device.** Each code registers a single unit and cannot be reused.
- **30-day window.** Registration must be completed within 30 days of purchase; a unit that is not registered in that window is not covered.
- **What you'll need.** The registration code from your warranty card and your proof of purchase.
- **How to register.** Register online at [pragoptics.com/#warranty](https://pragoptics.com/#warranty) — the address printed on your warranty card. Pick your device, enter your code, and leave a contact email. Creating an account is optional; registering the product is enough.

Registering ties your unit to your account so warranty service is fast and we can reach you about safety or field-relevant updates. See our privacy policy for how we handle your details.

## Transferring ownership

Warranties follow the **product**, not the first buyer. Any registered unit can be transferred to a new owner — sold, gifted, or handed down the crew — and the remaining coverage moves with it.

- **Human to human.** Neither side needs an account; email is all it takes.
- **How it works.** Transfer online at [pragoptics.com/#transfer](https://pragoptics.com/#transfer). A **claim code** goes to the email the products were registered under — proof the registration is yours. Select the units you're handing off, enter the new owner's email, and they confirm with their own code. Done.
- **What transfers.** The registration and all remaining coverage: the balance of the one-year defect cover and the lifetime case cover.
- **The registration email is the key.** Products are looked up by the email used at registration. Changed addresses yourself? Transfer to your own new email the same way.
